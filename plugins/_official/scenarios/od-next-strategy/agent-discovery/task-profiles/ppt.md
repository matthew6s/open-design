# Open Design Presentation execution profile v1

Use this profile to create or substantially redesign a presentation, slide
deck, pitch deck, keynote, or other pageable visual narrative. A summary,
speaker-note edit, or explanation without a requested deck is not enough.

Follow the ordinary Agent-turn orchestration supplied with this load. This
profile is strategy-neutral and does not activate OD Next v2.

## Resolve the story

Determine the audience, decision or outcome, speaking time or page budget,
source material, locked claims, narrative arc, data sources, brand references,
and requested format. One material clarification is appropriate when the
audience, central claim, or output format would change the whole deck.

## Deliverable and narrative

- Produce the requested editable source with a stable entry and an intentional
  slide order. Do not claim PPTX or PDF unless that file was actually created.
- Give each slide one dominant point. The sequence should establish context,
  tension or opportunity, evidence, recommendation, and next action as the
  subject requires—not merely divide prose into pages.
- Use concise, presentation-scale copy. A slide is a visual composition, not
  a document page; keep essential labels legible at normal presentation size.
- Preserve source truth. Cite or label important data, distinguish facts from
  assumptions, and never invent customer quotes, market data, or business
  results.

## Visual system

- Keep a consistent page grid, margins, type scale, palette, chart language,
  image treatment, and section rhythm.
- Vary composition to match the story while preserving clear family
  resemblance. Avoid a deck made from the same card grid on every slide.
- Use diagrams and charts only when they clarify a relationship. Labels,
  units, baselines, and comparisons must be truthful and readable.
- Prefer purposeful imagery and diagrams over decorative filler. Maintain
  accessible contrast and do not place essential information in cropped or
  obscured regions.

## Completion floor

- Every slide fits the requested viewport without overflow or clipping.
- Page order, navigation, and the main narrative are complete.
- Fonts and media resolve; charts and cited numbers agree with their sources.
- The actual deck entry opens and pages correctly when validation tooling is
  available.
