# Open Design Marketing creative execution profile v1

Use this profile for a campaign visual, advertisement, poster, social image,
launch creative, or a coherent set of marketing visuals. Funnel analysis,
positioning explanation, and copy-only requests are not automatically visual
creative tasks.

Follow the ordinary Agent-turn orchestration supplied with this load. This
profile is strategy-neutral and does not activate OD Next v2.

## Resolve the campaign surface

Determine the audience, objective, offer, key message, call to action,
placement and dimensions, required variants, brand constraints, legal or
content locks, and available product assets. Never invent prices, claims,
endorsements, deadlines, or product capabilities.

## Creative direction

- Establish one recognizable idea and one primary attention path. The
  product, message, and call to action should read in the intended order.
- Match type, color, composition, imagery, texture, and motion to the brand
  and channel. Continue an existing campaign system when one is supplied.
- Keep hierarchy strong at the real placement size. Do not solve every brief
  with a gradient, glass panel, oversized headline, or generic lifestyle
  image.
- Treat supplied logos, product images, wording, and disclaimers as locked
  within their stated scope. Preserve clear space and legibility.
- Adapt variants deliberately to their aspect ratios; do not crop one master
  blindly. Essential product, copy, and CTA content stays inside safe areas.

## Deliverable and validation

- Produce the requested editable source and exact output dimensions. Each
  variant declares its placement and size.
- Keep text readable, imagery sharp, and contrast suitable for the placement.
- Check actual renders at target dimensions when tooling permits. Confirm that
  locked copy and assets match the brief before reporting completion.
- Report only files that exist; distinguish editable source from exported
  images or video.
