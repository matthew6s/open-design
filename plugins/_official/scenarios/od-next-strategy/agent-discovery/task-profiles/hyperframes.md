# Open Design Motion execution profile v1

Use this profile for an animated sequence, motion-design composition, timed
product story, or video source with deliberate scenes and transitions. Video
summaries, subtitle extraction, and animation explanations are not motion
production tasks.

Follow the ordinary Agent-turn orchestration supplied with this load. This
profile is strategy-neutral and does not activate OD Next v2.

## Resolve the timeline

Determine the audience and channel, narrative goal, aspect ratio, duration,
scene order, locked script or copy, supplied media, voice/music requirements,
brand rules, and requested source/render outputs. Ask when a missing duration,
format, or locked narrative choice would invalidate the production route.

## Motion direction

- Build one visual world. Typography, palette, asset treatment, camera logic,
  transitions, and motion curves remain consistent across scenes.
- Give each scene one narrative job and enough time to be understood. Motion
  should express entrance, exit, emphasis, transition, causality, or spatial
  change—not serve as continuous decoration.
- Keep animated text readable. Respect platform UI safe areas and give copy
  enough hold time for its language and density.
- Align visual beats with voice, captions, music, and effects when present.
  The core story should remain understandable with sound off unless the user
  explicitly requests an audio-led piece.
- Avoid random camera shake, purposeless rapid cuts, continuous zoom, filler
  particles, and discontinuities in subject, lighting, scale, or style.

## Deliverable and validation

- Produce the declared editable timeline/source and only the render outputs
  the available route actually creates.
- Ensure duration, aspect ratio, scene order, captions, and locked copy match
  the brief.
- Validate the timeline/source and, when a renderer is available and requested,
  inspect the real rendered result. Never describe a final video as complete
  when only source or static frames exist.
- Report any unavailable renderer, codec, font, or media dependency exactly.
